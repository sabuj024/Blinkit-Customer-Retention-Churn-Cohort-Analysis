# Blinkit Customer Retention, Churn & Cohort Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding customer purchasing behaviour, retention patterns, and churn in a Blinkit-focused quick-commerce case study. The objective is to analyze order data, identify potential reasons for customer loss, and translate the findings into actionable business recommendations.

Using Python, SQL, and Excel, I explored customer churn, repeat purchases, cancellation reasons, revenue trends, and city-level customer patterns. The project also includes plans for an interactive Power BI dashboard and a business report to communicate the findings to stakeholders.

## Business Objectives

- Understand customer churn and repeat-purchase behaviour.
- Identify cancellation patterns and potential customer pain points.
- Analyze customer cohorts and revenue trends.
- Explore city-level churn patterns and retention opportunities.
- Develop business recommendations to support customer retention and customer experience improvement.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data cleaning and preparation using Pandas and NumPy.
- **SQL (MySQL):** Business queries, customer analysis, revenue trends, and churn analysis.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Reviewing query results and organizing analytical outcomes.
- **Power BI:** Planned interactive dashboard for business KPI monitoring and visualization.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Examined the raw dataset and its structure.
- Checked and removed exact duplicate records.
- Standardized text fields and prepared date columns.
- Reviewed missing values and data consistency.

### 2. SQL-Based Business Analysis
- Analyzed overall order and revenue performance.
- Identified customers with repeat delivered orders.
- Examined cancellation reasons.
- Explored churn patterns across cities.
- Grouped customers by their first delivered-order month for initial cohort analysis.

### 3. Customer Retention & Churn Analysis
The analysis focuses on understanding customer purchasing patterns, repeat orders, churn indicators, and potential retention opportunities. The results require careful interpretation where customer labels or dates are incomplete.

### 4. Data Visualization — Power BI
**Planned deliverable:** `Data_Visualization.pbix`

An interactive dashboard to present key business metrics, including customer churn, repeat purchases, monthly revenue, cancellation patterns, and city-level performance. The dashboard will help stakeholders identify areas that may require further investigation or targeted retention efforts.

### 5. Business Analysis Report
**Planned deliverable:** `Business_Analysis_Report.pdf`

A business-focused report summarizing the analytical findings, key performance indicators, business implications, and actionable recommendations. It will connect customer behaviour insights with potential strategies for improving retention and reducing customer loss.

## Key Business Questions

- How many customers place repeat orders?
- What cancellation reasons appear most frequently?
- Which cities have the highest number of customers flagged as churned?
- How does revenue change over time?
- How can cohort analysis help evaluate repeat-purchase behaviour?

## Business Value

This project demonstrates how customer and order data can support business analysis by helping identify potential retention opportunities, operational pain points, and customer behaviour patterns.

The intended business applications include targeted customer reactivation, improved delivery and payment experiences, and KPI-based monitoring of retention initiatives. These are potential recommendations that should be validated against reliable data before implementation.

## Important Data Note

This is an independent, Blinkit-focused analytical case study using a realistic dataset. The data should not be interpreted as verified internal Blinkit business data. Missing dates and inconsistent customer-level churn labels may affect certain findings, so these limitations should be considered when interpreting the results.
